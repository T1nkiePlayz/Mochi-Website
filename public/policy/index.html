Mochi

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mochi handles personal information when you use the Mochi website, account services, launcher, and related services.

Mochi is operated under the name Side Quest Games in Western Australia. We aim to handle personal information fairly, transparently, and in accordance with applicable Australian privacy law, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) where they apply.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Mochi, we may collect:

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, authentication status, and information needed to maintain your account.
  • Sign-in provider information: information returned by Google or GitHub when you choose to use one of those sign-in methods, such as your provider account identifier, name, email address, and profile image where supplied.
  • Profile information: information you choose to provide, such as a display name or avatar.
  • Mochi settings and metadata: launcher preferences, game names and descriptions, artwork references, categories, source information, launch targets or executable paths, Tofu configuration such as versions, runtimes, mod counts and status, and cloud-sync preferences where those features are enabled for your account.
  • Security information: information necessary to protect accounts, prevent abuse, and maintain authentication and security features, including authenticator-app two-factor authentication and passkey features where enabled.
  • Technical information: limited information such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, and diagnostic information may be processed by the services used to operate Mochi.

Mochi does not require a phone number to create or access an account.

2. How we use information

We use personal information only where reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • creating and maintaining your Mochi account;
  • providing sign-in, account security, and account recovery;
  • providing launcher and website features you request;
  • syncing supported settings and metadata when cloud features are enabled;
  • maintaining, securing, troubleshooting, and improving Mochi;
  • responding to support requests; and
  • complying with legal obligations or responding to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

Mochi does not use your game library to create advertising profiles. Game metadata may be processed to provide launcher features such as library organisation, artwork, categories, and optional cloud synchronisation.

3. Sign-in services and service providers

Mochi supports sign-in using Google, GitHub, magic links, and email and password. If you choose Google or GitHub, the relevant provider will process information according to its own privacy terms and policies.

Mochi also relies on third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate account, website, email, security, hosting, and related functionality. Those providers may process information on our behalf and may operate outside Australia. We seek to use providers appropriate to the services being delivered and take reasonable steps required by applicable law when personal information is disclosed overseas.

4. Cookies and local storage

Mochi may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies that are necessary to keep you signed in, remember preferences, support authentication, and maintain website functionality. We do not use these technologies for unrelated advertising profiling.

5. Cloud data, local storage, and game files

Mochi is designed to be local-first. The launcher does not need to upload complete game installations merely because you use an account. Where cloud features are available, they are intended for supported account information, settings, configurations, and metadata.

Some launcher data is stored locally on your device or in browser/application storage. This can include your local library, launcher preferences, appearance settings, behaviour settings, and optional IGDB configuration. Local data may remain on a device after you sign out until you remove or reset it.

When cloud synchronisation is used, supported metadata is associated with your Mochi account so it can be retrieved on another supported device. Cloud synchronisation does not mean that Mochi uploads complete game installations or arbitrary files from your computer.

You remain responsible for checking the permissions and terms of any third-party game, mod, platform, or service that you connect to Mochi.

6. Third-party metadata and integrations

Mochi may optionally connect directly to third-party services such as IGDB when you configure those services. An IGDB lookup can request game names, summaries, artwork, genres, and release information. Credentials used for optional IGDB lookups are kept in the local Mochi configuration and are not part of ordinary Mochi cloud synchronisation.

7. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information to service providers that help us operate Mochi, to authentication or sign-in providers you choose, where you ask us to do so, or where disclosure is required or authorised by law.

We do not disclose personal information to advertisers for the purpose of selling targeted advertising profiles.

8. Overseas processing

Some providers used to operate Mochi may store or process information outside Australia. The countries involved can change as providers and infrastructure change. Where Australian privacy law applies, we take reasonable steps to address overseas disclosure requirements.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should also protect your account credentials and devices.

10. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain your account and services, or where retention is required by law.

You can request deletion of your Mochi account and associated personal information. Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or maintain legitimate security records.

11. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable Australian privacy law.

12. Privacy complaints

If you believe Mochi has mishandled your personal information, please contact us first so we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve the issue.

For privacy enquiries, access, correction, deletion, or complaints, contact support@ashtontink.com.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Mochi's features, services, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version takes effect. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to make it noticeable to users.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@ashtontink.com.

Important: This policy is intended to describe Mochi's current privacy practices and is not a substitute for legal advice. If Mochi's business structure, data practices, or legal obligations change materially, this policy should be reviewed and updated.
